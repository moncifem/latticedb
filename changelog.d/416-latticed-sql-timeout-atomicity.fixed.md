`latticed --data-dir` (#345, #416, follow-up to #412): a connection that times out waiting for the database thread now also fails its open transaction block on the database side (`ExecSession::abort_transaction`), so a later `COMMIT` answers `ROLLBACK` and nothing of the block is committed. Before, a statement skipped or finished after its `57014` could still be committed with the rest of the block. A `57014` after the ceiling means the outcome is unknown for autocommit statements, `COMMIT` and DDL, which are not cancelled once dequeued; the README no longer says a statement ends at the ceiling at the latest, and no longer presents the 0700 directory check as a guarantee. `HostLimits` gains `wait_slack_ms`.
