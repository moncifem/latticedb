`latticed` installs its SIGTERM and SIGINT handlers before it opens the data directory. A stop signal during startup or recovery now ends the process at once with status 2 and a message on stderr, before the listening line is printed, so `docker stop` no longer waits for SIGKILL when the process is PID 1; before this it kept the default action and, as PID 1, ignored the signal. After 3 seconds of startup the process prints that a stop signal ends it. Recovery itself is not cancellable: the process exits and the next start recovers again. Not tested: the container image under `docker stop` during recovery, and Windows.
