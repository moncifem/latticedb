The driver smoke job now also runs pgjdbc 42.7.14 (JDBC, jar checked against a pinned sha256) and tokio-postgres 0.7.18 (own locked `Cargo.lock`) against latticed over loopback trust, with the same 17 steps as the other drivers. JDBC binds values as text; tokio-postgres has no text mode for parameters, so its parameter steps are pinned in `known.jsonl` against #608 (binary formats). The binary formats themselves are still not supported.
