The sync gateway limits what one principal can hold: `--gateway-max-connections-per-principal` (default 8, WebSocket and `POST /mcp` together), `--gateway-reserved-connections` (default 4 places of `--gateway-max-connections` kept for principals that hold none), `--gateway-max-long-polls-per-principal` (default 4) and `--gateway-max-long-polls` (default 16) for `lattice_subscribe` calls that wait. A connection or long poll over a limit gets `503` with `Retry-After: 1` before the upgrade or without waiting, and is counted. The output byte budget for slow readers (#529 part 2) and a per-principal executor budget are not done.
