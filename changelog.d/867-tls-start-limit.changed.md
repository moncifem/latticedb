`latticed` answers a TLS start over its rate limit with FATAL 53300 and a neutral text as the reply to the SSLRequest instead of closing the socket without a word, and the limits are settable: `--tls-starts-per-second` (default 64) and `--tls-starts-per-second-per-address` (default 16), each 1 to 10000, with `ServerConfig::tls_starts_per_second`, `tls_starts_per_second_per_address` and `ServerHandle::tls_start_refusals()`. Everything behind one address shares the per-address budget, which made tools that open one `psql` connection per statement, and CI runners, fail at random (#867). The dev stack raises both limits to 1000 when its image has the flags, and the board helper and console retry the new "error response during SSL exchange" connect failure and the text of the refusals. Defaults are unchanged, and the sync gateway has its own limits and is not affected.
